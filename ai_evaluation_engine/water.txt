SOUNDGUARD – AI-POWERED WATER PIPELINE LEAKAGE DETECTION SYSTEM

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
SoundGuard is an AI-powered acoustic water pipeline leakage detection and localization system designed to identify underground pipeline leaks at an early stage.
The system uses strategically placed acoustic/vibration sensors to capture abnormal sound signatures generated by pressurized water escaping from pipelines. Edge processing and machine-learning-based signal analysis classify potential leakage events, estimate their location, and generate alerts through a centralized monitoring dashboard.
The proposed pilot will cover approximately 20 km of water pipeline infrastructure.

2. PROBLEM
Water pipeline leakage is difficult to detect because a significant portion of the pipeline network is underground and leaks may remain undetected until visible damage occurs.
Conventional approaches often result in delayed detection, water loss, unnecessary excavation, higher maintenance costs, and road damage.

3. PROPOSED SOLUTION
SoundGuard proposes a distributed acoustic monitoring system consisting of:
- Acoustic/vibration sensors installed at selected pipeline locations
- Edge devices for signal acquisition and preprocessing
- Machine-learning-based leak classification
- Leak localization using signals from multiple sensor nodes
- Central monitoring dashboard with automated alerts

4. KEY DIFFERENTIATORS
- Continuous acoustic monitoring
- Multi-sensor leak localization
- Edge processing to reduce unnecessary data transmission
- Integration with municipal water utility SCADA
